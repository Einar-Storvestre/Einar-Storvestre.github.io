I developed NC AI Brief to explain developments in AI that are useful in study
and project work. My contribution covers research, source checking, selection
and editing, with AI assistance during production.

## The September issue

[Read the published issue](https://www.nhhsconsulting.no/ai/nyhetsbrev/september-2026)

The [launch article](https://www.nhhsconsulting.no/post/nc-ai-brief-forste-utgave/)
introduces the first issue. The [NC AI hub](https://www.nhhsconsulting.no/ai/)

The [NC website project](/projects/nhhs-consulting/) describes the publishing
infrastructure.
