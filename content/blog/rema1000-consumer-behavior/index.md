Our group examined consumer decisions involving milk, beer, coffee and the
REMA 1000 app. The report applies course concepts to a representative customer
persona and discusses how the purchase situations differ.

## The report

The analysis covers internal influences, attitudes and decisions, and the
social context of consumer behaviour. The full report includes the framework,
examples and references.
