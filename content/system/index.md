## Decisions that carry across projects

**Separate preparation from action.** A collected report can wait for delivery,
and a proposed appointment can wait for confirmation.

**Make uncertainty visible.** An unavailable review, missing source or ambiguous
booking result should produce a clear next step.

**Use an outcome suited to the job.** A successful process exit, a completed
sync and a delivered report are different signals.
